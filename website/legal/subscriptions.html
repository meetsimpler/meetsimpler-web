Beta Access and Future Subscription Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Current beta

MeetSimpler beta v0.1 is free. Registration does not ask for a payment card or create a paid subscription. Seven-day renewal is an entitlement refresh, not billing. Initial online activation is required; after seven days offline, reconnect so the server can check eligibility and issue another permit if the beta remains free. Optional AI-provider usage and internet/data costs are separate.

Later paid offerings

No paid checkout is available in this beta. If paid plans are introduced, the offer will state the price, currency, taxes, billing interval, renewal conditions, cancellation method, refund rules, device allowance and applicable withdrawal rights before purchase. Paid plans require your explicit agreement. Beta users are never charged merely because they registered.

Cancellation, refunds and access

There are no MeetSimpler beta subscription charges to cancel or refund. Any future paid plan will provide cancellation and refund terms before purchase, including how to stop future renewal charges. Statutory consumer remedies and withdrawal rights take priority.

Possible device limit

The current beta does not impose a two-device limit. Any future device limit will be disclosed with the plan, including how installations, replacements, reinstalls, device removal and account recovery are handled. Support: meetsimpler@gmail.com.
